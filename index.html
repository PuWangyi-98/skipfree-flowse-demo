Methods

Baselines were run with the authors' public code and checkpoints, default settings, and a fixed random seed (0). All models were trained on the VB-DMD training set at 16 kHz.

  • SGMSE+ [1]: J. Richter, S. Welker, J.-M. Lemercier, B. Lay, T. Gerkmann, “Speech enhancement and dereverberation with diffusion-based generative models,” IEEE/ACM TASLP, 2023. Official VB-DMD checkpoint (sp-uhh/sgmse, train_vb_29nqe0uh_epoch=115.ckpt), default predictor-corrector sampler with 30 steps and one corrector step (60 NFE).
  • FlowSE [2]: S. Lee, S. Cheong, S. Han, and J. W. Shin, “FlowSE: Flow matching-based speech enhancement,” ICASSP 2025. Official VB-DMD checkpoint (seongq/flowmse, VB_DMD_FLOWSE_ICASSP_2025.ckpt), Euler solver with 5 steps (5 NFE).
  • FlowSE (LRA, proposed): the skip-free waveform-domain backbone with latent representation alignment described in the paper, Euler solver with 5 steps (5 NFE).
